Good writing isn't the hard part. Being found is. A manuscript, a screenplay, a poem without visibility, none of it reaches the people who'd pay to read it, and work no one sees never earns a thing. What writers are missing isn't a place to write. It's a stage, and a straight line from that stage to a paying reader.

Everything a writer needs, in one place

From first draft to first sale Wilde replaces the spreadsheet of tools most writers cobble together with a single, purpose-built flow.

Create

A dedicated flow for every literary form; novels, screenplays, plays, poetry, long-form essays each with a professional-grade editor: chapters, industry formatting, and cover art.

AI-assisted creativity

Hit a block, and the built-in assistant points toward specific books, films, or works worth exploring for inspiration never handing over content, just pointing the way.

Publish & sell

One tap moves a finished work from draft to the Wilde Market, priced and ready to sell. No separate publishing pipeline, no waiting on anyone's approval.

Discover & connect

A feed of trending creatives and recent works, so readers discover writers the way they'd discover creators anywhere else except every piece here is purchasable.
